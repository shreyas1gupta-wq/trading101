# CFA L2 — Exam Strategy, Timing & Logistics

## Format (2026)
- **88 questions**, all in **item sets (vignettes)**: a case + 4–6 MCQs each.
- **Two sessions**, 44 questions each, ~**2 hours 12 minutes** per session, optional break between.
- Computer-based (CBT). On-screen calculator not allowed for finance math — bring your own approved calculator.

## Timing math (burn this in)
- 132 min ÷ 44 Q = **~3 min per question**, or **~18 min per 6-question vignette**.
- Plan: **~15 min reading + solving, ~3 min buffer** per vignette. If a vignette runs long, flag and move — never let one set eat another's time.
- Do a **time check every ~4 vignettes** (roughly every 30 min you should have cleared ~10 questions).

## How to attack a vignette (repeatable method)
1. **Skim the questions first** (10–15 sec) so you know what to hunt for in the case.
2. **Read the vignette actively**, underlining/noting numbers and the analyst's claims. Expect 1–2 **distractor facts** that aren't needed.
3. Answer **calculation questions** where the data is explicit first; leave interpretation questions for after.
4. For each MCQ, **compute the number AND confirm the direction/conclusion** — CFAI loves pairing a correct number with a wrong conclusion (and vice versa).
5. **Never leave blanks** — no penalty for wrong answers. Flag-and-guess, then return.

## Guessing & elimination
- Eliminate the obviously wrong option first → guess improves from 33% to 50%.
- If two options share a number and one shares a direction, the tested trap is usually the *direction* — reason it out.
- "Always/never" absolute answers in Ethics are usually wrong; "disclose / get written consent / act in client's interest" are usually right.

## Calculator — BA II Plus (set up before Monday)
- Set **P/Y = 1** and **C/Y = 1** (avoids TVM errors) unless a problem needs otherwise.
- Set **DEC = 4** (or floating) for precision.
- Know cold: TVM keys, CF/NPV/IRR, DATA/STAT (regression: enter X,Y → get slope, intercept, r), and 2ND→ quit/clear. Bring a **backup calculator + spare batteries** (only BA II Plus or HP 12C allowed).

## Topic triage on exam day
- Bank the points on **your strong, formula-driven topics first** (Equity, Derivatives, Corp Issuers) to build a cushion and confidence.
- Ethics and FSA reward careful reading — don't rush them; they're high weight.
- If stuck, remember the weights: Equity, FI, FSA, PM, Ethics = 50–75% of the exam. Protect time for those.
