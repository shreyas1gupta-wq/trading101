Self-contained study kit to take Neha from zero to exam-ready via chat-based teaching. Built and verified July 2026 against the official 2026 L2 curriculum (45 learning modules).

## Start here every session
Paste into any Claude session (Sonnet 5 or Opus):

**Day 1 goes live Monday 2026-07-20.** Say **"continue"** on Monday to begin Day 1 (Corporate Issuers).

## The approach in one paragraph
Zero prep, ~30 days out, fast learner (cleared L1 in 8 days on ~2,000 questions). Claude teaches each topic in chat — intuition → formal → worked example → exam traps — then ends every day with graded item sets, targeting ~2,000+ questions total. FSA and Quant (the weak areas) drip daily from Day 1 even though they're formally taught later. Everything commits to branch `claude/hopeful-dijkstra-tgopmr` so no session's work is ever lost.
