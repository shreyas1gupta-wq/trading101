# CFA L2 High-Yield Pattern Bank

_Repeatable exam patterns, most-tested concepts, and classic vignette structures. Claude appends to this during teaching whenever a "this is how they always test it" pattern appears. Drilled on Days 31, 34, and 39._

## Seed list (known heavily-tested L2 patterns — expand during study)

1. **FRA pricing & valuation** — price at initiation from spot rates, then value mid-life. Appears almost every exam.
2. **Pension cost components** — split P&L vs OCI under IFRS vs US GAAP; effect of assumption changes (discount rate ↑ → PBO ↓).
3. **Current rate vs temporal method** — which rate for which line item; where the translation G/L goes; ratio effects.
4. **FCFF/FCFE bridges** — from NI, EBIT, EBITDA, CFO. Sign errors on WCInv and FCInv are the trap.
5. **Binomial tree backward induction** — bond valuation, then callable/putable adjustment at each node.
6. **OAS logic** — OAS vs Z-spread, effect of volatility assumption on callable/putable OAS.
7. **Parity conditions triangle** — CIRP (always holds) vs UIRP/PPP (don't hold short-run); forward premium ↔ interest differential.
8. **Justified multiples** — P/E, P/B, P/S derived from GGM; what raises/lowers them.
9. **Residual income** — RI = NI − equity charge; V0 = B0 + PV(RI); clean surplus violations.
10. **Swap valuation mid-life** — value = difference in PV of remaining fixed vs floating (floating resets to par at payment dates).
11. **Equity method vs consolidation** — same net income, different revenue/assets/margins; ratio comparisons.
12. **Regression diagnostics** — identify heteroskedasticity / serial correlation / multicollinearity from symptoms; pick the correction.
13. **AR model checks** — covariance stationarity, unit root, seasonality (add seasonal lag), mean reversion level b0/(1−b1).
14. **REIT NAVPS / FFO / AFFO** — the standard adjustments table.
15. **Fundamental law of active management** — IR = TC · IC · √BR; expected active return.
16. **Ethics: Standard I–VII application** — material nonpublic info, fee disclosure, composite performance, supervisor duties.
