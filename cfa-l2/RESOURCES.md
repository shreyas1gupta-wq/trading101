## How this plan uses them (mandatory daily loop)
1. **Learn** the day's reading here in chat (concepts + worked examples).
2. **Drill** 2 Claude item sets (12 Qs) + 40–60 questions from **FreeFellow** (free) and/or **CFAI QBank** (official) on that exact topic. Log the score in `PROGRESS.md`.
3. **Mocks:** CFAI official mocks first (in LES); use the 300Hours free mock as an extra timed rep; Claude can generate a full custom mock on demand for reps 3–4.
4. **Weak flags** (< target score) resurface in the daily FSA/formula drip.
