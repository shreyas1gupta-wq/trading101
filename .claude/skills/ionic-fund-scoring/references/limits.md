# Known limits — read before quoting the score

## The score does not predict

The forward test runs 64 monthly formations and 28,376 observations. Rank correlation
between the score and the next twelve months of category-relative return is **+0.04**. In
the equity categories it is mildly **negative**. It is materially positive only in the
low-dispersion hybrid categories.

The score describes a record accurately and forecasts nothing. Every rationale string
states what **has** happened and never what will. Do not describe it as predictive, do not
annualise it, and do not let a deck imply the ranking is a forecast.

Supporting number: the universe distribution study covers 577 funds across 14 categories
with a three-year to five-year rank correlation of **+0.812**.

## Data and coverage limits

- **QFRA-1's data ends 2025-01-31** for five of six categories. It cannot originate a
  current Sell without a refresh. The bottom-third percentile rule is what actually
  produced the sells in the delivered book.
- **QFRA-2 covers 40 published funds** of 99 ranked. Its file conflict still needs the
  engine owner to declare which run is authoritative.
- **Sectoral and thematic funds get no score.** One AMFI bucket holds a defence fund
  beside a pharma fund; a percentile against that set would look precise and mean nothing.
- **Coverage.** The current file carries **17,904 ISIN rows; 2,875 carry a score**. The
  rest are debt, arbitrage, sectoral, or schemes without eight qualifying peers, and all
  say `No View`.
- **The NAV panel** is 138 month-ends, 2015-01 to 2026-07, after dropping months below 60%
  of peak scheme density. Verified: no gaps, no thin months, no lookahead.
